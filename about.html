About Premium Acrylics 415

Built by collectors, for collectors.

Premium Acrylics 415 started with a simple problem: sealed Pokémon and One Piece product is expensive, hard to find, and easy to damage just sitting on a shelf. Dust, sunlight, and the occasional bump can take a pristine box from mint to marked-down fast. We wanted a way to display our own collections without worrying about it — so we started cutting our own cases.

Every case we sell is precision cut from premium cast acrylic to fit a specific box, tin, or bundle exactly — not a generic "one size fits most" box. That means no rattling, no gaps, and a display that looks like it was made for the piece inside, because it was.

What we believe in

  • Precision over guesswork. We measure and cut for the actual box, not an approximation.
  • Honest shipping. We pass shipping through at cost — no markup, ever.
  • Collectors first. We're active in the TCG community and build what we'd want on our own shelves.

Got a box we don't carry yet?

We're always adding new sizes based on requests. If you have a box, tin, or bundle you'd like a custom case for, reach out — we regularly take custom orders.
