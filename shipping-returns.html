Shipping & Returns

Transparent pricing, no surprises.

Shipping

We ship every order at cost — exactly what it costs us to ship it to you, with no markup added. When you submit an order request, we'll follow up to confirm your exact shipping cost based on your address and the size of your order, before anything is charged.

All cases are carefully packaged to prevent shifting or damage in transit. If you have a special shipping request (expedited service, a specific carrier, international shipping, etc.), text us at 650-248-2473 before ordering and we'll do our best to accommodate it.

  • In-stock orders ship within 2–4 business days
  • You'll receive tracking information by email as soon as your order ships
  • Custom orders ship on a confirmed timeline discussed before you order

Local Pick-Up

Based in the South San Francisco area? Skip shipping entirely and pick up your order in person. Just mention local pick-up when you submit your order or text us, and we'll confirm your order with no shipping charge and text you when it's ready.

Pick-up address: 1158 Mission Rd, South San Francisco, CA 94080

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be happy with your case. If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 14 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement or issue a refund — your choice.

For change-of-mind returns, reach out within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Custom-cut cases are made to order and are final sale unless defective.

  • Contact us to start any return or exchange — we'll walk you through it
  • Damaged or incorrect items: full replacement or refund, on us
  • Change-of-mind returns: accepted within 14 days, item must be unused
  • Custom orders: final sale unless defective
