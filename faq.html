Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you order.

What are your cases made of?▾

Every case is cut from premium 5mm + 8mm cast acrylic — clear, rigid, and scratch-resistant. Cast acrylic holds its clarity far better over time than cheaper extruded plastic.

Will a case fit my exact box?▾

Each listing is cut for a specific box, tin, or bundle — check the product title and description for the exact fit. If you don't see your box listed, message us and we'll let you know if we can cut a custom size.

How is shipping calculated?▾

We charge shipping at cost — exactly what carriers charge us, with no markup. Submit an order and we'll confirm your exact shipping cost with you directly based on your address and order size, before anything is charged.

How long does an order take to ship?▾

In-stock cases typically ship within 2–4 business days. Custom orders may take longer — we'll confirm a timeline with you before starting.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?▾

Yes — see our full Shipping & Returns page for details. If something arrives damaged or isn't right, message us and we'll make it right.

Can I order a custom size?▾

Absolutely. Text us at 650-248-2473 or use the contact form with your box's dimensions and we'll quote you a custom cut.

How do I actually pay?▾

Submitting an order on the site sends us a request — it doesn't charge you anything automatically. We'll follow up by email or text to confirm your order and total (including shipping), and arrange payment from there.
